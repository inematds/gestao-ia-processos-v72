02 · From draft to signature

Four steps. The Horizonte canvas is the practice run; the one for your process is the one that counts.

1. Put it on one page

The canvas asks for nothing new: each block summarizes one or two pieces you have already made. The work here is to cut it down until it fits on one page, with the full pieces attached.

One page is a restriction on purpose. Whoever signs reads all of it, and six months from now you can remember in five minutes why you decided each thing.

At HorizonteCanvas "WhatsApp order → invoice · v2.0": five blocks on one page, and attached are the tagging sheet, the map, the redesign sheet, the executor chart, the indicator sheets, and change record no. 001.

2. Run it through the rubric

The rubric adds up to 100 points across seven blocks. You pass with 75 or more and the five mandatory items checked. One missing mandatory item fails you, even with 95 points.

The mandatory items come from the method's acceptance criterion: a named owner, an unambiguous trigger and completion, an indicator with a denominator, a baseline that is observed or marked as a hypothesis, and written scope exclusions.

Panel testRun the panel prompt (template alongside). It points out gaps; the score is decided by you and by whoever signs.

3. Who signs

Whoever built the canvas presents it; whoever answers for the process result signs. If they are the same person, the signature comes from whoever is above them, as in the promotion in lesson 7.

Before signing, the person listens to those who carry out the eliminated steps. They know what gets lost, and it is cheaper to find out beforehand.

At HorizontePatrícia builds and presents. Juliana and finance read the "how it will be" column. Rafael confirms what depends on integration. Carlos signs.

4. The bridge to the rest of the program

The approved canvas is the input for the other courses. The steps that stayed with an agent go to G3 (role, authority limit, supervision). The ones that stay with people go to G1. Cost and model go to G4.

In G5, the program's final project reuses the first two rubric items: a process with an owner, input, completion, and baseline, and steps cut before automating, with the reason.

At HorizonteSteps 1 and 2 of v2.0 belong to Hora: they become the role sheet and the authority limit matrix of G3. Patrícia's exception queue becomes the supervision routine.

Simulation: illustrative example

Order via WhatsApp → invoice · v1.0 → v2.0 · Horizonte, fictional study company · fictional data

  • Contract: owner Patrícia (backup: Juliana). Trigger: customer sends the order via WhatsApp. Completion: invoice issued and sent in the conversation. Result: order invoiced earlier and with less rework.
  • Current: 13 steps (H 2, P 4); rule decisions made late (registration only at invoicing); D1 incomplete registration (hypothesis: ~15 per week), D2 product out of stock.
  • Future: 8 steps; 5 eliminated (spreadsheet, paper, notice, email, retyping), checking everything becomes exceptions + 10 drawn at random, registration at intake. Agent in 2 steps, simple automation in 4, integration in 1, person in 6.
  • Scope: registered customers; left out at the start are new customer, exchange and return, sale by sales rep. Indicators: time to invoice (30 h, hypothesis → 24 h), returns (~9%, hypothesis → 3%), people-minutes per order (21, hypothesis → under 15), 8 weeks after v2.0. Guardrail: zero discounts outside the policy.
  • Version and risk: phase 1 registered customers up to R$ 5 thousand, phase 2 everyone. Goes back to v1.0 with returns above the baseline for 2 weeks or 1 discount outside the policy. Risk: ERP integration still without an answer from the vendor.

Rubric: 84/100, 5 of 5 required. Built by Patrícia, signed by Carlos.

  • Future without current. If the left column is empty, there is no way to prove that anything changed.
  • Hidden hypothesis. A value for today without the observed/hypothesis mark fails.
  • Scope without exclusions. "All orders" in the pilot mixes cases that behave differently.
  • Cut without a reason. Block 3 is worth 20 points: each cut with a reason and a new source.
  • More than one page. Details go in an attachment.

04 · What changed · October 2026

Quarterly panel, replaced at each course review. Each item with a date, a source, and the type of claim. The products are examples: the method in lessons 1 to 7 does not depend on any of them.

1. Tools for running processes

OpenAI: Workspace Agents, which replace Custom GPTs, are now billed per token (07/06/2026); ChatGPT Work delivers documents, spreadsheets, and slides (07/09/2026). AgentKit's Agent Builder will be shut down on 11/30/2026.

Anthropic: Managed Agents and, in Claude Code, background computer use (09/2026). Microsoft: Agent 365, announced at Build (06/2026). Sources: release notes and announcements from each vendor.

For the canvasTools get discontinued and change price within months. The executor chart records which tool does each step; changing tools is a version change, with a test and a way back (lesson 7).

2. Redesigning is what separates

Microsoft, Work Trend Index 2026 (05/2026, 20 thousand people in 10 countries, vendor research): 16% are Frontier Professionals, who use agents in multi-step workflows and redesign work; 58% do work they could not do a year ago, and among Frontier Professionals it is 80%.

IT Forum (opinion column, 2026): "the year companies stop building agents and start operating them".

ReadingThe numbers come from the people who sell the tools, but they point to the same thing as this course: the gain comes from changing the workflow, not from dropping the tool into the old workflow.

3. Brazil

KPMG Global Tech Report 2026 (04/2026): 45% of Brazilian executives report several disconnected AI projects. ABES and IDC (06/15/2026, 103 executives): 40% already invest in agents and 33% plan to within 12 months; obstacles cited: data, legacy systems, governance, and a shortage of professionals.

GFT, Tech Trends 2026 LATAM, with data from BCG: 18% of Brazilian companies integrate agents, versus 13% worldwide. Gartner (2026): only 17% of companies have agents in use.

Why the numbers don't match"Invest," "integrate," and "in use" measure different things, with different samples. Compare the definition before comparing the percentage.

4. How to read these reports

Who pays, and what do they sell? Vendors sell the solution to the problem they measure; consultancies sell the transformation they describe. How did they measure it: a survey with a sample, self-reported experience, or a forecast?

A Microsoft Cloud blog post reminds us that the WTI 2026 numbers shouldn't carry a business case on their own. Use the report as a map; the proof for your case is your own baseline.

Next review of this panelJanuary 2027, with the next quarter's overview. What is method (lessons 1 to 7) does not change; what is product and number does.
